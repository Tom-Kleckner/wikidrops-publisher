Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

This policy covers WikiDrops Publisher, a private workspace operated by the WikiDrops owner. It does not describe Postiz's separately operated hosted service. Contact: tiktokcancerchanel@gmail.com.

Information used by the workspace

  • Google and YouTube connection: the account's email and basic profile, channel identifiers and profile information, granted permissions, OAuth access and refresh tokens, and channel or video analytics retrieved through the connection.
  • TikTok connection, if enabled and authorized: account identifiers, username and display name, avatar, profile links, biography and verification status, profile statistics, public video metadata and engagement counts, granted permissions, and OAuth access and refresh tokens. TikTok is not currently connected; this describes the planned integration.
  • Publishing content: videos, images, text, titles, descriptions, visibility choices, schedules, drafts, and publishing results supplied by the owner or returned by the connected service.
  • Operation and security: workspace login records, authentication cookies, session information, and application error logs used to operate and troubleshoot the service.

The app uses YouTube API Services. Google sign-in happens with Google, and a future TikTok authorization would happen with TikTok. The workspace does not ask for or store your Google or TikTok password. The platforms explain their own data handling in the Google Privacy Policy and TikTok Privacy Policy.

Why and with whom information is used

Information is used to identify the connected channel, carry out authorized publishing instructions, show publishing status and analytics, maintain the connection, and troubleshoot failures. Data is accessible to the owner and expressly authorized operators. Content and requests needed for YouTube features are sent to Google and YouTube. If TikTok is enabled, the selected media, caption, publishing choices, and account authorization are sent to TikTok only for the authorized operation. A draft transfer may require the owner to finish publishing in TikTok.

WikiDrops does not sell connected-platform user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. The workspace's use and transfer of Google API information follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Workspace data is stored on the owner's privately operated server and computers. Restricted backups may contain the same data. The dashboard uses HTTPS and private network access; these controls do not eliminate all security risks.

Tokens are retained while the connection is needed and authorized. The operator checks retained YouTube API data at least every 30 days and refreshes or deletes it where required. Historical analytics may be retained while needed for the authorized purpose, with authorization checked at least every 30 days. Owner-supplied source media and working drafts are retained until the owner removes them or they are no longer needed.

If a TikTok connection is enabled, its API data is retained only as needed for the authorized function. When access ends or the data is no longer needed, the operator stops using it and removes the related tokens and data promptly, including affected backups.

Revoke access or request deletion

Revoke Google access at any time in Google account permissions. For TikTok, open Profile → Menu → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions, select the app, and choose Remove access. See TikTok's connected-app instructions.

To request deletion of stored account data, email tiktokcancerchanel@gmail.com and identify the connected account. Do not send passwords or tokens. Deletion requests are handled by the operator, including affected backups, within seven calendar days. After revocation through Google, related API data is removed within 30 days. Deleting workspace data does not delete content held by YouTube or TikTok; manage that content on the relevant platform.

Cookies and this information site

The private dashboard uses cookies and browser storage for sign-in and session operation. These static information pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, embedded players, or forms. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and protect the pages under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link opens a service with its own privacy practices.

Changes and questions

Updates will be posted here with a new effective date. A new use of connected-platform user data requires appropriate notice and consent. Contact the operator at the email above with privacy questions or complaints.
