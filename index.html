A publishing workspace for WikiDrops.

WikiDrops Publisher helps the WikiDrops team prepare videos and images, review drafts, schedule releases, and view channel analytics. It combines a self-hosted Postiz installation with content preparation tools. Access is available to the owner and authorized team members.

How the YouTube connection works

An authorized operator connects a Google account and chooses the YouTube channel to manage. With permission, the workspace uses YouTube API Services to upload content and retrieve channel information and analytics.

Google authorization does not publish a video by itself. Publishing requires a separate content instruction, including the intended channel and visibility. The owner can revoke the connection through Google account permissions.

TikTok integration

The TikTok workflow connects an authorized account, identifies the destination channel, and offers media and caption review before a publishing instruction. Postiz supports direct posting with the audience options TikTok permits, or uploading content for the account owner to finish in TikTok. These are separate from drafts saved only in the workspace.

Current availability: the connection uses TikTok's sandbox for an authorized test account. A video upload to TikTok's inbox was verified on October 18, 2026. Public Direct Post access has not been approved. Team access to the workspace does not determine a post's audience; platform permissions and the selected visibility do.

The privacy policy explains TikTok data use and how to withdraw access. Connecting an account alone never instructs the workspace to publish.
