A publishing workspace for WikiDrops.

WikiDrops Publisher helps the WikiDrops owner prepare videos, review drafts, schedule releases, and view channel analytics. It combines a privately hosted Postiz installation with the owner's content preparation tools.

How the YouTube connection works

The owner connects a Google account and chooses the YouTube channel to manage. With permission, the workspace uses YouTube API Services to upload content and retrieve channel information and analytics. Access is limited to the owner and people the owner authorizes. Public registration is not available.

Google authorization does not publish a video by itself. Publishing requires a separate content instruction, including the intended channel and visibility. The owner can revoke the connection through Google account permissions.

TikTok integration

TikTok is connected in sandbox mode for the owner's authorized test account. The connection identifies that account and requests access to its public videos, profile statistics, and the publishing features TikTok permits. Public video posting is unavailable in the sandbox, and media transfer has not yet been tested. The app is not approved for production.

The privacy policy explains TikTok data use and how to withdraw access. This site does not claim that TikTok has approved the app or that private-use publishing tools qualify for its production APIs.

About this site

This public site explains the app and its policies. The publishing dashboard and stored media remain on the owner's private network.
